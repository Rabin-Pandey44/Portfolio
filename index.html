A small habit, shared with care

What I Want To Do: The 1-Cigarette Swap

You smoke because nicotine tricks your brain into chasing a fake reward—a brief chemical illusion you buy by trading away your actual future. Think about it: what happens when your life later needs just a little more time to spend with the people you love? What if the version of you ten years from now desperately wants you to quit today, and that’s why this page is in front of you right now as a wake-up call? I am no one to force you or tell you how to live your life. Just think about it wisely.
This isn't about judging anyone or pretending change is easy. It's an invitation to pause, make one different choice, and see what that choice can do.


Come on! Let's do it... Why not?

Where This Journey Leads Next

I have a long way to go, and I want to be honest about that. Here’s the path I hope we can build—with care, community, and the people who know these needs best.

  1. 01
    Active — beginning here

    Street dogs, cared for with dignity

    Start with the 1-Cigarette Swap, street-dog food, and basic medical care in the community.

  2. 02
    Next step

    Show up for people who are often unseen

    Grow into direct outreach for people experiencing homelessness, listening first and working with local support.

  3. 03
    The dream I'm holding

    A place to begin again

    One day, help build a dedicated rehabilitation and recovery center in Nepal—where people facing addiction can find support, dignity, and a way to rebuild their lives.
